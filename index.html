Mastering Traditional Drafting from Zero

I am learning the rigorous discipline of classical drawing, technical illustration, and precision drafting completely from scratch. No digital crutches, no shortcuts—just pencil, vellum, ink, and a systematic 25-book curriculum.

Perspective Made Easy

  • Current Exercises: 1- and 2-point perspective grids, rotating box studies, inclined planes, and eye level sighting.
  • Target Competency: Placing rigid, volumetric 3D solids accurately in free space before moving to organic contour work.
