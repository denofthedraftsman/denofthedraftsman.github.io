Master Syllabus

The 6-Phase Curriculum

A sequenced, foundational progression: moving from basic spatial perspective and sighting, through graphite control and constructive anatomy, space composition, permanent ink, and industrial design markers, to precision engineering drafting.

Phase 1: Foundational Drawing, Form, and Perspective

Before mastering ink lines or marker values, you must master how to place 3D volumes into space accurately.

Texts 01 – 05

Provides the absolute simplest visual rules for 1- and 2-point perspective, rotating boxes, cylinders, and inclined planes, establishing the structural grid needed for everything else.

About Den of the Draftsman

Den of the Draftsman is dedicated to mastering traditional drafting, precision technical illustration, mechanical exploded views, and classical drawing disciplines.

Rather than keeping the learning process behind closed doors, every milestone across our 6-phase master curriculum is practiced in public—from early perspective grids to permanent ink on vellum and alcohol marker product rendering.
