About Den of the Draftsman

Den of the Draftsman is dedicated to the craft of precision technical illustration, mechanical exploded views, architectural elevations, and traditional drafting with technical pens on vellum.

Every piece is built from fundamental geometric projections, rigorous dimensioning, and classic hand drafting disciplines.
