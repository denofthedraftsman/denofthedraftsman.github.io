Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy outlines how Den of the Draftsman (denofthedraftsman.com) respects and protects visitor privacy.

1. Static Hosting & Zero Tracking

Den of the Draftsman is a high-performance static website hosted on GitHub Pages and protected by Cloudflare. We do not use third-party behavioral trackers, commercial ad retargeting pixels, or invasive surveillance scripts. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

2. Information Submitted via Inquiries

When you submit a message through our inquiry or commission forms, you voluntarily provide your name, email address, and project scope. This information is used solely to respond to your inquiry and communicate regarding requested drafting work. It is never added to unsolicited marketing lists.

3. Cloudflare Edge Security & Logs

Our domain routes through Cloudflare's network for SSL encryption, DDoS mitigation, and bot management. Cloudflare may process transient network data (such as IP addresses and request headers) strictly for security and performance purposes to block malicious scrapers and DDoS attacks, in accordance with Cloudflare's privacy policy.

4. Third-Party Links & Embeds

Our site includes links to our streaming and social channels (YouTube, Twitch, Twitter/X, Instagram, and Patreon). Clicking these links takes you to external platforms governed by their respective privacy terms and cookie practices.

5. Contact & Data Rights

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to request deletion of correspondence sent to us, contact us directly at privacy@denofthedraftsman.com.
