Analog Toolkit

Traditional Drafting Tools & Surfaces Guide

The exact analog instruments and archival papers utilized across the curriculum exercises.

Archival Paper Surfaces

  • 100% Rag Drafting Vellum: Transparent, dimensionally stable cotton-fiber paper designed for technical pen drafting and razor-blade scraping without surface fraying.
  • Strathmore 500 Series 2-Ply Bristol: 100% cotton plate finish for crisp pen-and-ink lines; medium/vellum finish for graphite value gradients and tooth.
  • Marker Layout Bond: Bleed-proof treated paper that holds alcohol ink on the surface for streak-free wet-into-wet blending.

Drafting Instruments & Inks

  • 2mm Leadholders & Clutch Pencils: Graded 4H/2H for precision perspective layout lines; HB/2B/4B for value zones and core shadows.
  • Technical Pens & Dip Nibs: Koh-I-Noor Rapidograph & Staedtler Mars Matic pens (0.18, 0.25, 0.35, 0.50mm) paired with waterproof black carbon India ink.
  • Geometric Alignment: Professional steel-edged T-squares, 30°/60° and 45° acrylic triangles, beam compasses, and ellipse templates (15° to 60° projections).
